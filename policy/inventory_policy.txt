INVENTORYIQ — STANDARD INVENTORY POLICY DOCUMENT
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Department: Supply Chain & Planning

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This document defines the standard inventory management policies applicable
to all stock-keeping units (SKUs) across all warehouses. It governs reorder
points, safety stock levels, minimum order quantities, lead times, and the
identification and treatment of slow-moving and obsolete inventory.

All planning and procurement staff are required to follow this policy.
Deviations must be approved in writing by the Supply Chain Manager.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. REORDER POINT (ROP) POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Definition
The Reorder Point (ROP) is the inventory level at which a replenishment
order must be placed to avoid a stockout before the next delivery arrives.

2.2 Standard Formula
ROP = (Average Daily Demand × Policy Lead Time in Days) + Safety Stock

Where:
  - Average Daily Demand  = Total units sold in last 90 days ÷ 90
  - Policy Lead Time      = Agreed lead time with supplier (in days)
  - Safety Stock          = Buffer stock to cover demand variability

2.3 Service Level Targets by E-Grocery Category
  - Fresh Produce:         99% service level (z-score = 2.33)
  - Dairy:                 99% service level (z-score = 2.33)
  - Meat:                  99% service level (z-score = 2.33)
  - Seafood:               99% service level (z-score = 2.33)
  - Bakery:                98% service level (z-score = 2.05)
  - Frozen:                97% service level (z-score = 1.88)
  - Beverages:             95% service level (z-score = 1.65)
  - Pantry:                95% service level (z-score = 1.65)
  - Personal Care:         95% service level (z-score = 1.65)
  - Household:             90% service level (z-score = 1.28)

Perishable categories must be reviewed with higher priority because service
failures can create both stockout risk and expiry/write-off risk.

2.4 ROP Review Frequency
  - High-velocity items (>100 units/month): Monthly review
  - Medium-velocity items (10–100 units/month): Quarterly review
  - Low-velocity items (<10 units/month): Semi-annual review

2.5 Lead Time Deviation Policy
If actual supplier lead time deviates from the agreed policy lead time by
more than 20%, the reorder point MUST be recalculated using the actual
observed lead time. Procurement must notify the supplier of the SLA breach
within 5 business days.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. SAFETY STOCK POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Standard Safety Stock Formula
Safety Stock = Z × σ_demand × √Lead Time

Where:
  - Z              = Service level z-score (see Section 2.3)
  - σ_demand       = Standard deviation of daily demand (last 90 days)
  - Lead Time      = Policy lead time in days

3.2 Minimum Safety Stock
Safety stock must not fall below 15% of the policy reorder point for any
active SKU, regardless of demand variability.

3.3 Safety Stock Override
Category managers may increase (but not decrease) safety stock by up to
50% above the formula output for seasonal or strategic items.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY (MOQ) POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 All purchase orders must meet the supplier-agreed MOQ.

4.2 If the calculated replenishment quantity falls below MOQ, the order
    must be rounded up to the nearest MOQ multiple.

4.3 MOQ exceptions require written approval from the procurement lead and
    will typically incur a premium charge from the supplier.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. SLOW-MOVING INVENTORY POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Definition
A SKU is classified as SLOW-MOVING if its average monthly sales velocity
falls below 10 units per month over a rolling 3-month period.

5.2 Identification
Slow-moving SKUs must be identified through automated velocity analysis
at least monthly. All slow-movers must be flagged in the planning system.

5.3 Required Actions for Slow-Movers
Upon identification, the category manager must within 30 days:
  (a) Review the commercial reason for the low velocity (e.g., seasonal,
      discontinued product, pricing issue, or demand shift)
  (b) Choose one of the following actions:
      - PROMOTE: Launch a clearance promotion or bundle offer
      - RETURN: Negotiate return to supplier under the return agreement
      - REPOSITION: Transfer to a warehouse with higher demand
      - HOLD: Document business reason and set a review date (max 60 days)

5.4 Replenishment Freeze
No new purchase orders may be placed for a SLOW-MOVING SKU without explicit
written approval from the Supply Chain Manager.

6. OBSOLETE INVENTORY POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 Definition
A SKU is classified as OBSOLETE if it meets ANY of the following criteria:
  (a) No stock movement (zero units issued or sold) for 90 or more
      consecutive calendar days.
  (b) The product has been formally discontinued by the manufacturer.
  (c) The SKU has been superseded by a replacement model and the old
      model has had no movement for 60+ days.

6.2 Mandatory Actions Timeline
  Day 0:   Obsolescence flag raised by system or planner.
  Day 1–7: Category manager must confirm the classification.
  Day 8–30: Finance must calculate the book value and provision requirement.
  Day 31–60: One of the following disposal actions must be initiated:
             - WRITE-DOWN: Reduce book value; sell at discount
             - WRITE-OFF: Zero the asset; dispose or destroy
             - SUPPLIER RETURN: Initiate return under warranty/return clause
             - DONATION: For eligible categories (e.g., Pharmaceuticals
               within expiry date)

6.3 Provisioning
Finance must raise a provision equal to 100% of the inventory book value
for any SKU classified as OBSOLETE within 30 days of classification.

6.4 No Replenishment
Absolutely no purchase orders may be raised for an OBSOLETE SKU.
Any such order requires VP Supply Chain approval.

7. POLICY COMPLIANCE MONITORING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

7.1 Compliance Score
Each SKU is assigned a Policy Compliance Score (0–100) computed monthly.
A score below 60 triggers a mandatory review. A score below 40 requires
escalation to the Supply Chain Manager within 5 business days.

7.2 Lead-Time Drift Monitoring
Supplier lead-time performance is reviewed monthly. Any supplier whose
average actual lead time exceeds the agreed lead time by more than 20%
for two consecutive months is placed on a Supplier Watch List.

7.3 Reporting
  - Monthly: Full compliance report to Supply Chain Manager
  - Quarterly: SLOB summary to Finance Director
  - Annually: Full policy review and update

8. SUPPLIER LEAD TIME POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

8.1 All supplier lead times are agreed at the start of each contract year
    and stored as the Policy Lead Time in the planning system.

8.2 Actual lead times are measured from Purchase Order date to Goods
    Receipt date and recorded in the system for every delivery.

8.3 Acceptable variance: Actual lead time may be up to +10% of policy
    lead time without triggering a review.

8.4 Breach thresholds:
  - 11–20% over policy lead time: Yellow flag — monitor next 2 deliveries
  - 21–50% over policy lead time: Orange flag — formal supplier review
  - >50% over policy lead time:   Red flag — escalate to procurement head;
                                  consider emergency sourcing

9. E-GROCERY EXPIRY AND FEFO POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

9.1 FEFO Requirement
Perishable SKUs must be picked, replenished, and rotated using FEFO
(First Expired, First Out). FEFO applies to Fresh Produce, Dairy, Meat,
Seafood, Bakery, and Frozen categories unless a category manager documents
an approved exception.

9.2 Expiry Risk Flags
Any SKU within 7 calendar days of expiry must be flagged for urgent action.
The category manager must choose one of the following actions:
  - PROMOTE: Launch markdown, bundle, or clearance activity
  - TRANSFER: Move stock to a warehouse or store with higher near-term demand
  - RETURN: Negotiate supplier return where contract terms allow
  - WRITE-DOWN: Start finance review for reduced recoverable value

Expired SKUs must not be sold, replenished, or counted as available stock.

9.3 Stock Age Review
Stock age must be reviewed weekly for perishable SKUs and monthly for
non-perishable SKUs. High stock age combined with low sales velocity should
be treated as an early warning for SLOB classification.

9.4 Inventory Status Rules
Items marked Low Stock, Out of Stock, Overstock, Damaged, or Expiry Risk
must be included in the monthly compliance review. Damaged or expired stock
must be excluded from reorder-point availability calculations.

10. GLOSSARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ROP      Reorder Point — stock level triggering a new purchase order
MOQ      Minimum Order Quantity — smallest quantity supplier will accept
SLOB     Slow-Moving and Obsolete Stock
SKU      Stock-Keeping Unit — unique product identifier
SLA      Service Level Agreement — agreed lead time with supplier
z-score  Statistical factor corresponding to a desired service level
FEFO     First Expired, First Out — stock rotation by earliest expiry date
FIFO     First In, First Out — stock rotation by earliest receipt date
